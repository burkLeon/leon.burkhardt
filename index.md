I am a PhD student in the Topological Data Analysis group at
[KTH](https://www.kth.se/) in Stockholm under the supervision of Associate Professor [Martina Scolamiero](https://www.kth.se/profile/scola). My position is funded by [digital futures](https://www.digitalfutures.kth.se/). I also have an [institutional website](https://www.kth.se/profile/leonbu).


My research is part of a transdisciplinary project aimed at improving our understanding of gene regulatory networks, which play a central role in controlling cellular processes and biological function. My work lies at the intersection of topological data analysis, dynamics, and network science, with a particular focus on understanding the mechanisms that govern the shape and structure of gene regulatory networks and how such networks can be simulated. From a mathematical perspective, I study questions related to random topology and the expected persistence features of random networks. 


More broadly, I like to research interconnections between different areas, be it about phenomenons from nature, languages or mathematics. I am also really enjoy thinking about how to communicate science and in particular mathematics to a broader audience. Within Mathematics, my main interests lie in algebraic topology, dynamics, and applications.
